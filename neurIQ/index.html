The Problem

Most learning tools treat every brain the same.

Generic courses, content you forget within a week, and dashboards full of numbers that never tell you what to do next. The result is wasted hours and stalled motivation.

📉

You forget faster than you learn

Without spaced repetition timed to your forgetting curve, most new material is gone within days.

🎯

One-size-fits-all content

Courses ignore your pace, attention span and prior knowledge — so you're either bored or lost, rarely in flow.

🧩

Learning ≠ doing

Watching lectures doesn't build a portfolio. Without real projects, knowledge never becomes a demonstrable skill.

Under the Hood

Production-grade engineering, not a prototype.

NeurIQ is a full-stack monorepo with a dedicated cognitive-analytics microservice. Type safety, accessibility, bilingual i18n and defensive null-handling are baked into the codebase standards — not bolted on later.

  • Async, queued AI generation with job polling & resilient fallbacks
  • Cognitive analytics isolated in a Python service for clean separation
  • Light/dark theming, EN/TR i18n and a custom design system
  • Playwright E2E plus unit tests across client and server

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who is NeurIQ for?

STEM, CS and AI students, plus self-taught developers — anyone who wants to learn faster and actually retain it. It's especially friendly to learners who struggle with focus and consistency.

What exactly is a "cognitive profile"?

A live model of how you learn — your attention, pace, review needs and preferred difficulty — built from signals like response time, accuracy and session length. It powers every recommendation in the app.

How is this different from other AI learning apps?

Most stop at personalization and dashboards. NeurIQ stacks three engines together — cognitive profiling, project-based reality and visual skill trees — so learning ends in a real, portfolio-ready artifact.

Is it free during beta?

Yes. The private beta is completely free. The focus right now is validating value and gathering deep feedback before introducing any pricing.

How fast is course generation?

Around ten seconds for a full course — a 1-week plan, modules, a quiz and flashcards — generated asynchronously so you can keep working while it builds.
